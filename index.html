About Me

Hi, I’m Preeti, the creator behind PreetyCreation24, based in Pune.

My journey started with a love for creativity and jewellery, which inspired me to create beautiful and unique pieces for women who love to express their style.

At PreetyCreation24, we create a variety of trendy and stylish earrings that are perfect for everyday wear as well as special occasions. We also offer customised jewellery, so you can share your idea, colour, design or style with us and we’ll try to turn your vision into something beautiful.

For us, every piece is more than just jewellery — it’s a small creation made with love, creativity and attention to detail.

“The love and support you have given me until now has been my biggest motivation. I sincerely thank each and every customer who has supported PreetyCreation24. I hope you continue to shower the same love and support on our journey ahead, from Pune and beyond.” ✨
